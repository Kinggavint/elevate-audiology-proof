Common questions about the Easley office

Where is Elevate Audiology located in Easley, SC?

Our Easley office is at 424 Rock Springs Road, Easley, SC 29642. We are off Rock Springs Road just north of downtown Easley, about ten minutes from the Highway 123 corridor. Free parking is available on site.

What are your hours at the Easley office?

Our Easley office is open Monday through Thursday from 8:30 AM to 4:00 PM and Friday from 8:30 AM to 1:00 PM. We are closed on weekends. Call (864) 447-6611 to schedule.

Do you accept new patients at the Easley office?

Yes. We accept new patients of all ages. Adults can schedule a comprehensive hearing test directly. For children, we recommend a parent or guardian call ahead to discuss the right type of evaluation.

What insurance does the Easley office accept?

We work with most major insurance plans. Coverage for hearing tests, hearing aids, and tinnitus care varies by plan. Call our Easley office at (864) 447-6611 and our Patient Care Coordinators will verify your benefits before your visit.

Do you treat tinnitus at the Easley office?

Yes. Our Easley office offers full tinnitus evaluation and a range of treatments including FDA-approved Lenire bimodal neuromodulation therapy, hearing aids with built-in tinnitus support, and counseling-based approaches.

Can children get auditory processing testing in Easley?

Yes. We perform Auditory Processing Disorder evaluations in our Easley office for children age seven and up, and for adults. We use the Bellis and Ferre model and provide a written report you can share with schools and other providers.
