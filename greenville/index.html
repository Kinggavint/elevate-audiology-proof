Greenville audiology FAQs

Do I need a referral to see an audiologist in Greenville?

In most cases, no. You can book directly with our clinic. If your insurance plan requires a referral, we can tell you what is needed when you schedule.

What happens at a hearing test appointment?

We start by talking about your concerns, then check your ears and run hearing tests in a sound booth. You leave with clear results and next steps.

Can you help with tinnitus?

Yes. We offer tinnitus evaluations and care plans that may include counseling, sound therapy, and hearing devices when appropriate.

Do you treat earwax buildup and offer earwax removal?

Yes. If earwax is blocking the ear canal, we can assess it and remove it safely when appropriate. If you have pain, drainage, or sudden hearing loss, call us so we can guide you to the right next step.

Do you fit and service hearing aids?

Yes. We help you choose hearing aids, program them to your hearing needs, and provide follow-up care like adjustments and repairs.

How long does an appointment take?

Most evaluations take about 60 minutes. Some visits are shorter or longer depending on what you need.

Where are you located?

We serve patients in Greenville and the Upstate. When you schedule, we will confirm the best location and directions.

Earwax removal FAQs

Do you remove earwax in the office?

Yes. We can check for earwax buildup and remove it safely when it is appropriate.

Should I use cotton swabs to remove earwax?

No. Cotton swabs can push wax deeper and can irritate the ear canal. It is safer to have your ears checked first.

When should I schedule an appointment for earwax removal?

If you have muffled hearing, ear fullness, or your hearing aid seems blocked, schedule a visit so we can look in your ears and guide the next step.

Do you sell hearing aids in Greenville?
