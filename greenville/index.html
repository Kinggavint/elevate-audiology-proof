Greenville audiology FAQs

Answers about appointments, hearing care, insurance, and our Greenville office.

Where is Elevate Audiology located in Greenville, SC?

Our Greenville office is at 2086 Woodruff Rd Suite B, Greenville, SC 29607, on the Woodruff Road corridor near I-385. It is easy to reach from downtown Greenville, Simpsonville, Mauldin, and Five Forks. Free on-site parking is available.

What are your Greenville office hours?

Our Greenville office is open Monday through Thursday from 8:30 AM to 4:00 PM and Friday from 8:30 AM to 1:00 PM. We are closed on weekends.

Do I need a referral to see an audiologist in Greenville?

In most cases, no. You can book directly with our clinic. If your insurance plan requires a referral, we can explain what is needed when you schedule.

Do you accept new patients at the Greenville office?

Yes. We accept new patients of all ages. Adults can schedule a comprehensive hearing test directly. For children, we recommend that a parent or guardian call ahead so we can discuss the appropriate evaluation.

What happens at a hearing test appointment, and how long does it take?

We begin by discussing your concerns, check your ears, and perform hearing tests in a sound booth. You leave with clear results and recommended next steps. Most comprehensive evaluations take about 60 minutes, although appointment length varies by service.

Do you fit and service hearing aids in Greenville?

Yes. We provide hearing evaluations, help you select hearing aids based on your hearing profile and lifestyle, program and verify them for your needs, and provide follow-up adjustments and repairs. In many cases, we can also evaluate and service hearing aids you already own.

What hearing aid brands do you carry?

We work with leading hearing aid manufacturers rather than defaulting to one brand. We recommend options based on your hearing test results, daily listening environments, and personal preferences.

Can hearing aids help with tinnitus?

For many patients, yes. Some hearing aids include tinnitus-management features, and improving access to everyday sounds can make tinnitus less disruptive. Your audiologist can determine whether hearing aids should be part of your tinnitus care plan.

Do you treat tinnitus at the Greenville office?

Yes. Our Greenville office provides tinnitus evaluations and individualized care that may include counseling-based approaches, sound therapy, hearing aids with tinnitus support, and FDA-cleared Lenire bimodal neuromodulation therapy when appropriate.

Do you treat earwax buildup and offer earwax removal?

Yes. We can assess earwax buildup and remove it safely when appropriate. Do not use cotton swabs, which can push wax deeper or irritate the ear canal. If you have pain, drainage, or sudden hearing loss, call us so we can guide you to the appropriate next step.

Can children get auditory processing testing in Greenville?

Yes. We perform Auditory Processing Disorder evaluations in our Greenville office for children age seven and older and for adults. We use the Bellis and Ferre model and provide a written report that can be shared with schools and other providers.

What insurance does the Greenville office accept?

We can file insurance claims for hearing aids and related services upon request. Coverage depends on your plan's in-network or out-of-network benefits. We are not providers for managed care plans. Call (864) 447-4183 with questions about your benefits.

How do I schedule an appointment at the Greenville office?

Call the Greenville office at (864) 447-4183 or schedule an appointment online.
