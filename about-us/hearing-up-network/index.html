About the Hearing Up Network

Launched by Dr. Cliff Olson, audiologist and founder of Applied Hearing Solutions in Phoenix, Arizona, the Hearing Up Network brings together audiologists who follow the Hearing Up approach to hearing care. Dr. Olson and his team hand-pick audiologists from across the country who meet the standards of his proven methods.

Membership in the network signals that a practice follows verified best practices, including:

  • Real Ear Measurements (REM) on every hearing aid fitting
  • Comprehensive diagnostic testing beyond a basic hearing screen
  • Personalized counseling and follow-up care
  • A commitment to ongoing education and evidence-based practice

To learn more about the Hearing Up Network and Dr. Tarvin's involvement, visit hearingup.com.
