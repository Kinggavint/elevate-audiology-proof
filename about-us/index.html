About Elevate Audiology

Independent. Evidence-based. Built around you.

A locally owned Hearing & Tinnitus Center serving the Upstate of South Carolina from Offices in Easley and Greenville, telehealth throughout South Carolina

Welcome to Elevate Audiology.

Home of Dr. Alexandra Tarvin, Dr. Katelyn Ooms, Dr. Khori Cioli, and Dr. Scott Capece, our practice exists for a single reason: to help the people of the Upstate hear, connect, and live more fully.

We are a small, locally owned, independent business including board-certified Doctors of Audiology, tinnitus and sound-sensitivity specialists, and auditory processing experts who are committed to YOU and continuously learning for YOU.

Our patients come to us because their physicians trust us, their families trust us, and we take the time to do audiology care the right way.

What we stand for
