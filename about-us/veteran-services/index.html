Honored to Serve Those Who Served

Hearing Care for Veterans Through VA Community Care

At Elevate Audiology — Hearing & Tinnitus Center, we are honored to provide hearing care services for eligible Veterans through participation in the VA Community Care Network. Our goal is to make high-quality audiology care more accessible locally, so Veterans can receive support closer to home when they are authorized for community care.

A Personal Connection

Local Hearing Care with a Personal Connection to Service

Caring for Veterans is personal to our team. Dr. Alexandra Tarvin's husband, Brennan, served in the United States Air Force, and several members of our team have close family members who have served in the military.

We understand that hearing concerns, tinnitus, and communication challenges can have a significant impact on daily life, relationships, work, and overall well-being. Our team is committed to providing thoughtful, evidence-based care in a welcoming and supportive environment.

What We Provide

Services May Include

Depending on the authorized referral and individual needs, Veterans may be seen for services such as:

Comprehensive Hearing Evaluations

Diagnostic testing to fully assess your hearing health.

Hearing Aid Evaluations & Fittings

Selection and custom fitting of hearing aids matched to your needs.

Hearing Aid Follow-Up & Adjustments

Ongoing support to keep your devices performing at their best.

Tinnitus Evaluations & Counseling

Evidence-based assessment and support for ringing in the ears.

Support for Communication Challenges

Strategies and tools to improve listening in everyday situations.

Custom Hearing Protection

Available when appropriate and authorized as part of your care.

All care provided through VA Community Care must follow the referral and authorization process.

Getting Started

How to Access Care

Veterans who are interested in being seen at Elevate Audiology through VA Community Care should contact their VA care team to discuss eligibility and referral options.

  1. Contact your VA care team to discuss eligibility for community care.
  2. Request a referral to Elevate Audiology — Hearing & Tinnitus Center.
  3. Once a referral and authorization are received, our team will help coordinate scheduling and provide clear information about your appointment.
Our Commitment

Grateful to Serve Veterans in Our Community

Our mission is to provide clear answers, honest guidance, and individualized hearing care that helps each patient feel informed, supported, and empowered. Elevate Audiology — Hearing & Tinnitus Center participates in VA Community Care.
