Hearing evaluation FAQ

How long does a hearing evaluation take?

Most visits take about 60 to 90 minutes. We will review your history, look in your ears, and run hearing tests.

Do I need a referral?

Most patients do not need a referral. If your insurance requires one, our team can tell you what to bring.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Bring your ID, insurance card, and a list of medications. If you have past hearing tests, bring those too.

Will you check for earwax or ear infections?

Yes. We look in your ears as part of the visit and explain what we see.

What happens after the test?

We explain your results in plain language and go over next steps. If hearing aids are a fit, we will review options and pricing.

Do you see patients in Easley and Greenville?

Yes. You can request an appointment for our Easley office or our Greenville office.
