The Elevated Hearing Pathway™

Most Clinics Only Test Your Hearing. We Test How You Hear in the Real World.

Standard hearing care gives you an audiogram and a device. We go further. The Elevated Hearing Pathway includes six additional real-world assessments that reveal how your hearing actually functions in the environments that matter to you — at dinner, at work, with your grandkids.

1

Personalized Hearing Status Questionnaires™

Connects your experience to real-life situations — family, work, and social conversations. Reviewed by your audiologist before your appointment so your care is personalized from the start.

2

Speech-in-Noise Reality Check™

Reveals why you may hear but still struggle to understand in noisy settings — and guides tailored recommendations for the right technology or strategies to improve real-world listening.

3

Binaural Brainpower Testing™

Measures how well your brain uses both ears together to follow conversations — a more complete picture of your auditory system than standard single-ear testing.

4

Comfort Zone Hearing Profile™

Finds your safe and comfortable listening range, and identifies how your personality influences your abilities in background noise.

5

Clarity Review™

A clear, step-by-step explanation of your results in everyday language — with personalized recommendations on whether hearing aids, monitoring, further testing, or other solutions are best.

6

Family Communication Toolkit™

Simple, practical tools to improve conversations at home, with or without hearing technology. Reduces frustration and misunderstandings for you and your loved ones right away.
