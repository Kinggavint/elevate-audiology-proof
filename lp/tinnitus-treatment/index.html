FDA-Approved Bimodal Neuromodulation

Meet Lenire® — The Device That Targets the Brain, Not Just the Ears.

Lenire is an FDA-approved medical device that helps reduce the severity of tinnitus by retraining the brain's response to internal noise. It works through bimodal neuromodulation — using customized sound through Bluetooth headphones and gentle electrical pulses to the tongue. These signals work together to change the way the brain processes and responds to tinnitus over time.

Unlike many treatments that simply mask the sound of tinnitus, Lenire targets the neurological roots of the condition. The goal is not just to help you cope, but to help you pay less attention to the tinnitus altogether — making it less noticeable and less distressing in daily life.
