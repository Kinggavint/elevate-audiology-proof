Oticon<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Oticon\u2019s history dates back to 1904. The company was founded by Hans Demant, whose wife struggled with hearing impairment. Oticon is headquartered in Denmark, just outside of Copenhagen, and has more than 3,000 employees in branches across the globe. Oticon\u2019s BrainHearing\u2122 technology allows you to hear better with less effort. Popular products include the Intent, Real, More, and Opn.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Phonak<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Phonak, founded in Zurich, Switzerland in 1947, employs more than 4,200 people in multiple locations worldwide. The company is a pioneer in hearing loss solutions, developing the first hearing aid to take advantage of Bluetooth\u00ae technology in 2003 and the first extended-wear hearing aid (Lyric) in 2011. Its Hear the World initiative, launched in 2006, aims to create global awareness of hearing loss and promote solutions for individuals across the planet. Featured products include the Infinio, Lumity, Paradise, and Marvel.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

ReSound<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

GN ReSound is a Danish manufacturer with headquarters in Ballerup. Founded in 1943, the company currently has 4,100 employees worldwide and its products are available in more than 80 countries. ReSound was the first to offer Wide Dynamic Range Compression and Digital Feedback Suppression, innovations in sound processing that have led to reduced distortion and better sound quality. Its product lineup includes the Vivia, Nexia, OMNIA, Key, LiNX Quattro, and ENZO.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Widex<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Widex was founded in Denmark in 1956 and is still owned and operated by relatives of the original founders. It employs more than 4,000 people worldwide and sells product in more than 100 countries. Widex created the world\u2019s first digital in-the-ear hearing aid and remains on the forefront of technology with breakthroughs in wireless connectivity. Products include the Allure, SmartRIC, Moment, and Evoke.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Signia<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

The Sivantos Group is a Singapore-based company with 5,000 employees in more than 25 countries. They have been manufacturing hearing aids since 1913, when production began in Berlin. With a focus on research and development backed by quality German engineering, Siemens has been on the cutting-edge of technology. They introduced the first behind-the-ear model in 1959 and continue to develop innovative products. In 2015, Signia was launched as a more forward thinking and customer centered brand. Their extensive lineup includes the Styletto, Insio, Pure Charge and Go, and Silk.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Starkey<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Starkey Hearing Technologies was founded in 1967 by William Austin, who believed in the concept of bringing people together in order to change the world. They are based in Eden Prairie, Minnesota, and are the only American-owned major hearing aid manufacturer in the world. Starkey employs 5,000 people and has 21 locations in 18 countries. Their Starkey Hearing Foundation donates a portion of all proceeds to help those in need across the globe receive hearing assistance. Their lineup includes Edge AI, Genesis AI, Evolv AI, and Livio.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Unitron<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Founded in 1964, Unitron is a Canada-based hearing aid manufacturer that focuses on a legacy of excellence, commitment to its product design and customer satisfaction. Unitron offers a learning program known as FLEX, which helps hearing aid providers and audiology practices make the journey of hearing care more flexible and accessible for patients worldwide.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Sonic Innovations<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Founded in 1998, Sonic Innovations grew out of an effort from three scientists to create the best completely digital hearing aid. The company continues to refine and work upon its innovations, which place an emphasis on reducing background noise and how the human brain perceives sounds.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
