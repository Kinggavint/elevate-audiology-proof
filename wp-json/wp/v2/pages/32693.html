    3. What Does the APD Evaluation Involve?<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

    We complete a comprehensive evaluation that looks at both hearing<\/strong> and how the brain processes sound<\/strong>.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    The process includes:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

      \n
    • Intake call and detailed questionnaires<\/li>\n\n\n\n
    • Hearing health diagnostics (to rule out hearing loss)<\/li>\n\n\n\n
    • A customized test battery (based on age, history and symptoms) that may include:\n
        \n
      • Auditory attention<\/li>\n\n\n\n
      • Sound discrimination<\/li>\n\n\n\n
      • Binaural integration and separation<\/li>\n\n\n\n
      • Auditory closure<\/li>\n\n\n\n
      • Pattern recognition<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n

        After testing, you\u2019ll receive a comprehensive report<\/strong> and a review call to discuss results, diagnosis (if applicable) and personalized recommendations.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

        4. What Therapy or Treatment Options Are Available?<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

        Therapy recommendations are tailored to the individual and may include:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

          \n
        • In-office one-on-one therapy sessions<\/li>\n\n\n\n
        • Virtual one-on-one therapy sessions<\/li>\n\n\n\n
        • Home-based, online therapy programs<\/li>\n\n\n\n
        • Environmental modifications (home, school or workplace)<\/li>\n\n\n\n
        • Compensatory listening strategies<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n

          Dr. Ooms will discuss the best approach based on evaluation results.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

          Therapy costs vary depending on the type and number of programs<\/strong> recommended. Pricing will be reviewed in detail after the evaluation and individualized treatment plan are created.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

            \n
          • Children<\/strong>: We evaluate children age 7 and older<\/strong> for APD. <\/li>\n\n\n\n
          • Adults<\/strong>: We evaluate adults up to age 64<\/strong>.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n

            7. Do I Need a Referral?<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

            No referral is required. If you have had a recent hearing evaluation (within the last 6 months), please share it with us before your appointment.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

            8. I Don\u2019t Live Near Your Office. Can I Still Be Tested?<\/h2>\n\n\n\n
