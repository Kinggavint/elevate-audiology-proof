What is Lenire?

Bimodal neuromodulation for tinnitus.

Lenire is an evidence-based treatment device proven to soothe tinnitus that can be used from the comfort of your home with assistance from qualified healthcare professionals. The device plays customized sounds through headphones and provides gentle pulses to the tongue via a Tonguetip™ — retraining your brain to pay less attention to your tinnitus.

Both Dr. Tarvin and Dr. Cioli have completed advanced, specialized training in tinnitus management and the Lenire system, working alongside some of the leading experts in the field. Because dispensing the Lenire device requires rigorous, specialized training, we are proud to offer this innovative treatment option to our qualifying patients.

How It Works

Three components, one coordinated treatment.

01

Bluetooth Headphones

Provided with the system. Deliver customized sounds to activate the auditory nerve to the brain. Settings are tailored to you by your healthcare professional.

02

Tonguetip™

Specifically designed to treat tinnitus. Sits comfortably in the closed mouth. Tiny electrodes deliver mild, safe energy pulses to the surface of the tip of the tongue.

03

Handheld Controller

Lightweight controller manages timing and intensity. Start, pause, or resume sessions, adjust the sound volume, and adjust the level of tongue stimulation.

The Research

What the data says.

Lenire’s grant of FDA approval followed a large-scale clinical trial designed specifically to meet FDA requirements. In that trial:

  • 83% of patients recommended Lenire for the treatment of tinnitus
  • The results were confirmed by real-world evidence from 204 additional patients
  • Lenire is the first device of its kind to be cleared by the FDA for tinnitus treatment

Getting Started

How to know if Lenire is right for you.

Lenire isn’t the right fit for every patient — and that’s exactly why it requires a thorough evaluation. Here’s the process.

Step 1

Contact our office

Tell us about your tinnitus and goals. We’ll learn more about your needs and schedule the right evaluation.

Step 2

Comprehensive evaluation

If you don’t have complete and thorough hearing test results from an audiologist within the past 6 months, we’ll perform a full hearing evaluation as part of your visit.

Step 3

Lenire fitting & follow-ups

If we mutually decide you’re a good fit, we’ll schedule your Lenire fitting and the appropriate follow-ups to begin treatment. Some follow-up visits can be completed by phone or telehealth.
