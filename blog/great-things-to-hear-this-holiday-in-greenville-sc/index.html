Great Things to Hear This Holiday in Greenville, SC

At Elevate Audiology, our team is committed to reconnecting you to the people and sounds in your life. We know how important it is to reconnect with your family and loved ones during the holidays, and we are here to make sure that you don’t miss a single sound. If you’ve been struggling with hearing loss, this is the time to give us a call. This holiday season, there are so many exciting things to hear around Greenville, South Carolina. Here are some of our picks for festive family events this month.

Charles Dickens’s A Christmas Carol

The Logos Theatre. December 14, 25, 21, 22. 7pm. $34-47.50

A holiday classic, A Christmas Carol is an age-old favorite for people of all ages. This renowned Charles Dickens tale of a selfish man, Ebenezer Scrooge, learning to open up during the holidays through a tour with Ghosts of Christmas Past, Present, and Future, is not to be missed at The Logos Theatre. It is a story of community, love, and redemption and captures the spirit of giving during the holiday season.

Polar Express Party

At various Greenville Libraries (December 12: Travelers Rest; December 12: Fountain Inn; December 14: Taylors)

Inspired by the beautiful children’s book The Polar Express by renowned author Chris Van Allsburg, Greenville Libraries’ Polar Express Party is a delight for the entire family. Set in Grand Rapids, Michigan (Van Allsburg’s home town), The Polar Express tells the story of a young boy who wakes up on the night of Christmas Eve to a surprise – but rather than Santa, it is a train that is waiting for him. Children and adults alike enjoy the boy’s magical journey through forests and mountains to the North Pole in the adventure of a lifetime. Crafts and snacks are provided.

The Nutcracker

Greenville Symphony Orchestra. December 21-23, Peace Concert Hall.

The Nutcracker is a favorite holiday tradition for many. Follow the story of young Clara as she sneaks down on Christmas night to check on her beloved nutcracker doll, only to get wrapped up in a whirlwind, whimsical adventure with the Nutcracker Prince. They travel through the Land of Snow and the Land of Sweets, meeting the Sugar Plum Fairy, and facing off with the Mouse King. With the International Ballet Company and the Greenville Symphony Orchestra, this iteration of the classic tale is sure to be a new family favorite.

Christmas Inn Our Town

Fountain Inn. December 6-22, Wednesday-Sunday, 6pm-9pm.

Take part in the awesome holiday activities at Fountain Inn with Christmas Inn Our Town! This annual favorite includes a carriage ride (must be reserved beforehand), a market with local vendors to pick up last minute holiday gifts, activities for the entire family, and a beloved tree lighting.

It’s a Wonderful Life

Greenville Little Theatre. December 13-15, 7:30pm. $20-28.

Based on the classic Frank Capra film, It’s a Wonderful Life tells the story of George Bailey, a down-and-out man who is uplifted by the appearance of his guardian angel, Clarence Odbody. Through this fantasy journey, George Bailey learns just how important the giving spirit is during the holiday – and how important he is to his community and loved ones. Greenville Little Theatre’s performance of this beloved tale is sure to be a family-pleaser.

All I Want for Christmas Are My Two Front Teeth

Greenville Little Theatre, December 11, 7pm; December 12-14, 10:30am; December 15, 10:30 am and 2pm.

For the little ones in your family (children ages 3-10), Greenville Little Theatre brings a production (possibly inspired by the Chipmunks’ hilarious holiday song) of All I Want for Christmas Are My Two Front Teeth. When second-grader Lucy Lesprit loses her two front teeth before a big school talent show, where she intended to showcase her whistling abilities, she makes a Christmas wish! A hilarious and heartwarming comedy for the whole family.
