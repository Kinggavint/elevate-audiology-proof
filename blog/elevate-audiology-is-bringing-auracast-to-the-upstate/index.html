Elevate Audiology Is Bringing Auracast to the Upstate

October 2026 | Elevate Audiology News

At Elevate Audiology, our mission has always gone beyond the exam room. That's why we're working to expand Auracast technology into public spaces throughout Easley, Greenville, and the surrounding Upstate.

Auracast is a next-generation Bluetooth standard that lets compatible hearing aids and devices stream audio directly from TVs, speakers, and public address systems in places like venues, houses of worship, and community spaces — making it easier for people with hearing loss to stay connected wherever they go.

This is part of a broader commitment we've made to the Upstate: complimentary services at Samaritan Health Clinic of Pickens County, active membership in the Greenville and Easley Chambers of Commerce, and ongoing support for local organizations and initiatives through our time, talent, and treasure.

We'll share updates here as new Auracast locations go live. If your organization is interested in bringing Auracast to your venue, please contact us at info@elevateaudiology.com.
