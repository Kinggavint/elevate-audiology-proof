Hearing aid batteries

The right battery, every time.

From disposable zinc-air to rechargeable lithium-ion, here's everything you need to know about powering your hearing aids — including which size you need and how to make them last.

Buy Batteries at Our Office
Sizes & color codes

Disposable zinc-air battery sizes.

Hearing aid batteries are color- and number-coded so they're easy to identify. The size you need depends on your hearing aid's style.

SizeColorTypical use
10YellowSmallest CIC and ITC hearing aids
13OrangeBTE, RIC, and ITE hearing aids
312BrownRIC and ITE — the most common size
675BluePower BTE for severe-to-profound loss

Zinc-air batteries activate when you remove the protective seal — wait at least one minute before inserting so they fully absorb oxygen.

Rechargeable batteries

Why most patients are switching to rechargeable.

Rechargeable hearing aids are now one of the most-requested features at Elevate. They free you from the daily hassle of changing tiny batteries, performing battery tests, and always carrying spares.

They're also better for the environment — a single rechargeable hearing aid saves nearly 100 disposable batteries per year from landfill.

  • Up to 24 hours of hearing on one charge
  • Fast-charge options available on most models
  • Charger doubles as overnight storage
  • No more spare batteries to keep track of
  • Significantly reduced environmental impact
Tips

Get the most out of your batteries.

  • Store at room temperature — heat, cold, and humidity all shorten battery life
  • Skip the refrigerator — condensation and temperature swings damage batteries
  • Keep them away from metal — keys and coins can short-circuit a battery
  • Open the door at night — turn aids off and open the compartment to prevent moisture buildup
  • Watch the expiration date when buying in stores
  • Listen for the warning — most aids beep when the battery is low
  • If sound stops — your first suspect should always be a dead battery
  • Recycle with us — bring used batteries to your next visit
