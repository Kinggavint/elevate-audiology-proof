Insurance and Financing

Insurance and Financing.

We believe in evidence-based care without cutting corners. You pay for the services you need to get to a thorough diagnosis and the right plan — and we tell you exactly what those services are.

Insurance

How we work with insurance.

We are in-network with Medicare. We are out-of-network with all other insurance plans.

Other than medically-necessary Medicare submissions, payment is due at the time of service. As a courtesy, we'll file with your insurance on your behalf so you can pursue any out-of-network reimbursement available to you.

Have insurance questions? Contact us

  • In-network with Medicare
  • Out-of-network with all other insurance
  • Courtesy filing on your behalf
  • Payment due at time of service
  • Transparent, itemized fees

Financial assistance

Financial Assistance

If you are motivated to improve your quality of life, we are motivated to serve you. Elevate Audiology is committed to helping our community.

Please do not let fear of cost stop you from making an appointment with us. Financing options for treatment plans are available for those that qualify.

In addition, we are proud Vocational Rehabilitation and Starkey HearNow providers, as well. These options are only available to those that qualify. Due to limited availability, if you intend to utilize an assistance program, please inform us during your initial phone call.

  • Vocational Rehabilitation partner provider
  • Starkey HearNow provider
  • Financing for those who qualify
  • Treatment plan options
  • Tell us during your first call
What's always included

Every patient receives proper care.

Whichever payment model you choose, every hearing aid plan at Elevate Audiology comes with the following.

  • Professional fitting with Real Ear Measurement
  • Hearing aid maintenance and counseling
  • Bluetooth connectivity setup (when applicable)
  • 30 days of follow-up
  • Rechargeable option (when applicable)
  • Cleaning and care kit
  • Manufacturer repair warranty
  • Loss & damage protection insurance

Have a question about cost or insurance?

We're happy to walk through how Medicare or your insurance plan applies before you schedule.
