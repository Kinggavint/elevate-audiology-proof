Hearing test FAQs

How long does a hearing test take?

Most appointments take about 45 to 60 minutes, depending on your history and what tests we run.

Do I need a referral for a hearing test?

No. You can schedule directly with Elevate Audiology. If you have insurance questions, our team can help before your visit.

What happens during the test?

We review your concerns, look in your ears, and measure how you hear tones and speech in a sound-treated space.

Can you test for tinnitus?

Yes. We can measure your hearing and talk through tinnitus triggers, and then explain options that may help you feel better.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Bring a list of medications, any prior hearing test results you have, and your hearing aids if you currently wear them.
