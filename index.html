Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Elevate Audiology

Quick answers from our Doctors of Audiology in Easley and Greenville, South Carolina.

Who are the audiologists at Elevate Audiology?

Our team is led by Dr. Alexandra Tarvin, Au.D., owner and founder of Elevate Audiology. Dr. Tarvin is joined by Dr. Khori Cioli, Dr. Katelyn Ooms, and Dr. Scott Capece. Every provider is a Doctor of Audiology. Dr. Tarvin was the first audiologist in South Carolina certified to deliver Lenire tinnitus treatment.

Where are your offices?

We have two locations in the Upstate of South Carolina. Our Easley office is at 424 Rock Springs Road, Easley, SC 29642, phone (864) 447-6611. Our Greenville office is at 2086 Woodruff Rd Suite B, Greenville, SC 29607, phone (864) 447-4183. Both offices are open Monday through Thursday 8:30 AM to 4:00 PM and Friday 8:30 AM to 1:00 PM.

What services do you offer?

We provide diagnostic hearing testing, hearing aid fitting and programming with Real-Ear Measurement, tinnitus evaluation and treatment including FDA-approved Lenire, auditory processing disorder (APD) evaluations for children and adults, earwax removal, and custom hearing protection. We fit hearing aids from eight major manufacturers including Oticon, Phonak, ReSound, Starkey, Signia, Widex, Unitron, and Sonic.

Do you treat tinnitus?

Yes. Tinnitus care is a clinical focus of our practice. We offer a full tinnitus evaluation followed by an individualized plan that may include FDA-approved Lenire bimodal neuromodulation, prescription hearing aids with tinnitus features, sound therapy, counseling, and long-term follow-up. Our team was trained on Lenire in the second U.S. cohort in 2023 and has participated in Lenire-related research.

What is Lenire and does it work?

Lenire is the first FDA-approved bimodal neuromodulation device for tinnitus. It pairs gentle sound through headphones with mild electrical pulses delivered to the tongue. In clinical trials, 83 percent of patients said they would recommend it. Treatment is usually 12 weeks of daily 30 to 60 minute sessions at home, with check-ins at our clinic.

How much does a hearing test cost?

A standard diagnostic hearing evaluation may be covered by insurance, discounted, or private pay. Our Elevate Hearing Pathway, which adds six advanced assessments to reveal how you hear in real-world environments, is a separate cost and only $99 in addition to any standard diagnostic exam cost. Our Patient Care Coordinators provide expected costs for your services upon scheduling.

Do you accept insurance?

We are in network with Medicare Part B, offer discounts for Medicare Advantage plans are out self-pay for all other insurances. We are happy to courtesy file billable charges to insurance. While out of pocket, we often find self-pay pricing to be lower than deductible and co-insurance for many different policies. Specialty services like tinnitus, APD, misophonia, and hyperacusis are not often covered by any insurance carrier regardless of in-network eligibility.

Do you see children?

Yes. We perform pediatric hearing evaluations and auditory processing disorder testing for children age seven and up. For very young children, please call the office to discuss the right type of evaluation before scheduling.
