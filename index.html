Our Approach to Tinnitus

The Elevate Audiology Approach to Tinnitus: There is hope, there is help!

While many people experience tinnitus without significant disruption to their lives, for others it can be overwhelming and deeply affect daily functioning. At Elevate Audiology, we understand that every experience with tinnitus is unique—and so is the care it requires.

Our approach begins with truly getting to know you: your symptoms, your hearing history, the health of your auditory system and how tinnitus is impacting your life. From there, we work together—as a team of experienced audiologists and an engaged, informed patient—to build a personalized plan for relief.

We offer a full range of evidence-based options, including simple daily strategies, FDA-approved devices and exclusive access to a proprietary tinnitus app developed by Dr. Tarvin and her husband. You are not alone in this—our team is here to guide and support you every step of the way.

Schedule your tinnitus appointment today and take the first step toward clarity, calm and control.

Our Approach to Hearing

The Elevate Audiology Approach to Hearing: The Elevate Hearing PathwayTM

Our care is much more than a hearing test—it’s your roadmap to clarity, confidence and better hearing outcomes.

The best hearing loss treatment is customized to your unique needs and lifestyle. At Elevate Audiology, a hearing test is just the start of this process. Our approach, The Elevate Hearing PathwayTM, includes:

  • A hearing status questionnaire that connects your experience to real-life situations.
  • A speech-in-noise reality check that reveals why you may hear but struggle to understand in noisy settings.
  • Binaural brainpower testing that measures how well your brain uses both ears together to follow conversations.
  • A comfort zone hearing profile that finds your safe and comfortable listening range.
  • A clarity that explains your results and provides personalized care recommendations.
  • A family communication toolkit that focuses on improving conversations at home.

Throughout this process, our team will learn about your symptoms and how they impact your daily life. We will then build a personalized plan of care and support you through every step.

Schedule your first appointment today to stay engaged with your world’s most meaningful sounds.
