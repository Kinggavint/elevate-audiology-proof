Welcome to Elevate Audiology

Hearing care that puts you first.

Welcome to Elevate Audiology — Hearing & Tinnitus Center, home of Dr. Alexandra Tarvin, Dr. Katelyn Ooms, Dr. Khori Cioli, and Dr. Scott Capece. We are a small, locally owned, independent practice committed to evidence-based, patient-first care for the Upstate of South Carolina.
